# Art direction

16px world grid, nearest-neighbour textures, 640x360 internal viewport, integer viewport scaling. Character animation sheets use 32px frames with four direction rows; static NPC sheets use 16px cells. Source-pack sprites retain their native size; larger objects use their original multi-tile atlas rectangles.

Village: warm grass, honey paths, timber cottages and garden accents. Forest: rich green conifers, river blue and a distinctive broad oak. Mountains: cool pale snow, dark blue chasm and transient white-blue wind wisps. Volcano: charcoal ground, ochre embers and orange lava, with cyan reflected fire for clear allegiance. Kitchen: warm wood, parchment tones and a preparation counter.

Chef costume: ivory toque, apron and coat, red neckerchief, warm skin, blue-grey metal spatula. Costume overlays follow movement bob, jump height and directional swings. Custom SVG puzzle objects use hard pixel-grid edges and the pack's warm outlined palette. Keep future content in this family; do not mix unrelated pixel densities.

The current visual pass supplies all required prototype gameplay objects, including letter, three ingredients, stones, furnace emblems, spout, gate, counter and bridge. Effects and the pigeon are code-drawn. Further hand-authored environmental detail, more nuanced chef direction/pose artwork and balance feedback remain worthwhile polish work.
