A Royal Feast

Double-click RoyalFeast.exe. Keep RoyalFeast.pck beside it.
WASD move; mouse aim; left click whack/reflect; E interact; Shift dodge; Space jump; right click throw; H hint; J journal; M map; Escape pause.

A native Godot prototype. The unmodified supplied Godot executable is bundled as the runtime; a normal release-template export can be made from the included source.
